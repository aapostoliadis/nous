# Medium image captions and alt text

All six images are 3200 × 2240 PNG screenshots of the running Nous prototype. The project and its source material are fictional. No real research findings are implied.

## (1) 01-workspace.png

**Placement:** Lead image, or after “Chat is chronological. Work is not.”

**Caption:** The workspace makes the goal, context and relationships visible. Natural language directs the work; persistent objects hold it.

**Alt text:** A warm paper-coloured workspace with a shared learning-space goal, six connected evidence, assumption, claim, question, decision and artifact objects, a context inspector and a natural-language command bar.

## (2) 02-branching.png

**Placement:** After “Make branching a first-class interaction.”

**Caption:** Alternatives remain available as independent branches, each preserving its own objects and decisions.

**Alt text:** Three parallel directions for a neighbourhood learning space: small shared beginnings, an online commons and a permanent home, each with preserved objects and a distinct exploration state.

## (3) 03-provenance.png

**Placement:** After “Treat provenance as interaction.”

**Caption:** A claim can be followed through supporting or contradictory evidence to the original material. The limits of the evidence stay visible.

**Alt text:** Supporting and contradictory fictional interview excerpts appear side by side, with the original interview notes open in the source inspector.

## (4) 04-decisions.png

**Placement:** After “Decisions need state.”

**Caption:** A decision is more than a recommendation. Its current state, connected objects and reason for change are part of the record.

**Alt text:** A decision view distinguishes two accepted directions from a rejected alternative. The selected six-week pilot shows its accepted state and a recorded rationale in the inspector.

## (5) 05-document.png

**Placement:** After “Support multiple views over the same objects.”

**Caption:** Change the representation, keep the work. The document uses the same claims and decisions as the map, with their identities and states intact.

**Alt text:** The pilot brief presents the workspace goal, claim and accepted pilot decision as a readable document, while the same decision remains open in the inspector.

## (6) 06-action-review.png

**Placement:** After “Design a separate surface for action.”

**Caption:** Before an action runs, the user can inspect its inputs, scope, destination and impact. This prototype demonstrates approval with a real local brief download.

**Alt text:** A proposed action to prepare the pilot brief displays its inputs, local destination, expected change and preview, with separate approve and decline controls.


## (7) Space to see the whole map

File: `07-fullscreen-map.png` (2880 × 1900 pixels).

Caption: Expand the map to fill the screen. The objects scale together to fit the available space without scrollbars.

Alt text: Fullscreen map with six connected objects, a visible goal and branch selector, and an exit control at the bottom-right.
